FAQ

Questions? Answers.

The things people ask us most before they start a project.

How does the pricing work?

Every website starts at ₹99. Select your website options and features in the planner; the displayed total is the price for your selected website and features. Website care, maintenance, and other standalone services are not included and must be purchased separately. Custom domains, paid hosting, and third-party charges are separate unless explicitly included.

How do I pay?

We share payment details after your order is reviewed. Work begins after we receive an 85% advance. When development is complete, we show you a website preview for review. The remaining 15% must be paid before final handover.

How long will my website take?

The minimum required delivery time is 4 days. The timeline may be longer depending on your selected features and website complexity. After reviewing your order, we will email you the expected delivery timeline. Delays in receiving content or feedback may extend it.

What do I need to give you?

Your business name, logo, text, photos, contact details and any references you like. Don't have everything ready? Choose Content formatting help in the planner and we will help arrange what you have.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Each project includes 2 rounds of revisions within the agreed scope. Please group your requested edits into each round. New features, major changes to the agreed scope, or extra revision rounds may be charged separately.

Do I need to buy a domain and hosting?

A website needs hosting and may use a custom domain. If you choose a custom domain or paid hosting, you are responsible for the registration, subscription, and renewal charges. We can help set these up, and the related third-party costs are separate unless explicitly included.

What if I'm not happy?

If you request a refund before work starts, 80% of the amount paid will be refunded. No refunds are available once work has started. Our full policy is in section 6 of our Terms & Refund Policy. Bugs in delivered work are fixed for 15 days after handover.

Terms & Refund Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Our services

AROM DIGITALS designs and builds websites based on the plan and features selected by the client. The price displayed in the website planner is the price for the selected website plan and features. Website care, maintenance, and other standalone services are not included in that price and must be purchased separately. Custom domains, paid hosting, and third-party charges are additional unless explicitly included.

2. Payment

  • Work begins after we receive an 85% advance payment.
  • When development is complete, we will show the client a preview of the website for review.
  • The remaining 15% must be paid before final handover of the website.
  • Taxes and third-party charges (including domain, hosting, payment gateway fees, paid plugins or subscriptions) are separate unless explicitly included.

3. Delivery time

The minimum required delivery time is 4 days. The timeline may extend depending on the selected features and complexity of the website. After reviewing the order, AROM DIGITALS will email the expected delivery timeline. Delays in receiving required content, images or feedback may extend the delivery date.

4. Revisions

Each project includes 2 rounds of revisions within the agreed scope. Clients should group requested edits into each round. New features, major changes beyond the agreed scope, or additional revision rounds may be charged separately.

5. Client responsibilities

The client must provide accurate content, images and logos, and confirm that they have the right to use them. AROM DIGITALS is not responsible for copyright issues in material supplied by the client.

6. Refunds

  • Before work starts: if a refund is requested after payment but before work begins, 80% of the amount paid will be refunded.
  • After work has started: no refunds will be provided.
  • After delivery: no refunds are available. Bugs in the delivered work will be fixed for 15 days after handover.
  • Approved refunds are processed within 7-10 working days to the original payment method.

7. Domain and hosting

If the client chooses a custom domain or paid hosting, the client is responsible for the purchase, subscription, and renewal charges. AROM DIGITALS can assist with setup when requested, but these costs are separate from the website price unless explicitly included.

8. Third-party services

Domain, hosting, payment gateways and other third-party integrations are subject to their providers’ own terms, fees and policies. Availability and performance may depend on those providers.

9. Ownership

After full payment, the client owns the final website files. AROM DIGITALS may show the completed project in its portfolio unless the client objects in writing.

10. Your information

The details you submit are used to prepare your quote and contact you about your project. If you submit a review, your name, business name and review is published on our website. We may remove reviews that are fake, abusive or spam. Your email or phone number is never published.

11. Website care plans

AROM Care plans are separate from the one-time website-build price. Essential Care is ₹499 per month or ₹5,499 per year and includes up to 3 small website changes per month. Growth Care is ₹899 per month or ₹9,999 per year and includes up to 5 small website changes per month, plus weekly checks and WhatsApp support during business hours. Yearly plans are paid upfront for 12 months. Small changes are minor updates to existing content or sections; new pages, major redesigns, new integrations and advanced development may require a separate quote. Unused monthly changes do not roll over. Care checks and support do not guarantee uninterrupted uptime and may depend on hosting and other third-party providers. Domain, hosting, renewal and third-party fees are separate unless agreed in writing. A website care request submitted through this site does not initiate automatic payment or auto-renewal; the plan activation and payment arrangement will be confirmed separately.
